# Part 3 · The sources

For Kamiya a source can be a person, an activity, a ritual, a value, a belief or a hope, as long as it brings ikigai-kan. One question per message; mirror, ground, then ask.

## Questions
1. Look at the moments you described. What made them possible: a person, something you did, a place, something you believe?
2. What else in your life gives you that feeling, even a little? Small things count.
   (repeat until there are three to seven sources)
3. For each source, one at a time: what feeling does it bring you?
4. Which of these sources would you miss most?
5. Is there a source you used to have and lost, or that is fading?

Done when: three to seven sources, each with its feeling in the user's words.

## Fragility check
Ask directly, one question: "Does any of these depend on one role or one person?" Record the answer in `sources[].fragile` (`yes`, `no`, or `not asked`). Ask permission before sharing anything you notice yourself.
- Does one source carry most of the feeling?
- Does a source depend on one role (a job, a title) or one person?
- Are all sources of one kind (all work, all family)?
Kamiya's line: several sources make the feeling sturdier.

## Categories (for your map, not to lecture)
people · activities and crafts · rituals and small pleasures · values and beliefs · hopes and future goals · contribution (being needed)

## Record in the profile
`sources`: list of `{name, kind, feeling, origin_of_wish (optional), fragile (yes/no, the user's view)}`.
