# Follow-up session (after four to six weeks)

Insight without experience fades. The follow-up turns the first step into learning and the map into a living thing.

## Before you start
Ask the user to paste or upload the profile (`ikigai-profile.json`). If they cannot, say you do not have it and ask for a short recap in their own words; never fill in its contents yourself. Do the state check from `safety.md`. Ask how the past weeks were in general before going to the step.

## 1 · The first step
- Did you take it? If yes: what happened, what did you notice in your energy, body, thoughts? If no: what got in the way? (No judgement: a step not taken is information too. Was it too big, the wrong moment, or not really yours?)

## 2 · The feeling
Ask for a recent moment when life felt worth living, and which source brought it. Compare with the moments in the profile.

## 3 · Re-score the seven needs
Same scale, same questions, short. Show the change next to the first scores. Ask what moved and why.

## 4 · Check the verdicts (only if Part 5 was done)
For the chosen direction and any candidate that came up again: run a short version of the submodality check (`submodalities.md`, step 3 and 4 only). Did the image move closer, gain colour, feel lighter? Or the reverse? The user decides what that means.

## 5 · Next step
Three routes, the user chooses:
- **Deepen:** a slightly bigger step in the same direction.
- **Adjust:** the core was right, the form was not; reshape the step.
- **Switch:** the direction turned out to be borrowed or not alive; go back to the candidates.

Define the next step with the same rule as before (this week, small, reversible, real experience).

## 6 · Update the map
Before updating, copy the first scores to `first_needs` (once). Then put the new scores in `needs`, add a `follow_ups` entry (`date`, `step_result`, `decision`, `next_step`) and re-render the map; the first scores show as a dashed outline. Offer the next reflection in four to six weeks.

## Splitting the first session
If the user is tired or time is short, a natural split is: session 1 = parts 0 to 3 (standpoint, the feeling, the sources), session 2 = parts 4 to 6 (seven needs, optional owned or borrowed, map and step). Save the profile between sessions.
