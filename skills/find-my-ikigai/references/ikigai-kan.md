# Part 2 · The feeling (ikigai-kan)

The heart of Kamiya's work. One question per message. Mirror, then ground (see `grounding.md`), then the next question.

## Questions (each item is one message; the "next" lines come in later messages)

1. Think of a recent, ordinary moment when you felt: this is worth it, this is my life. What was happening?
   (next: What did you feel in that moment? Name the feelings.)
   (next: Where in your body did you feel it?)
2. And a moment like that from longer ago?
3. Was there a hard period in which you still felt, at moments, that life was worth it? What gave you that then?
   Skip if the state check was low or the user signals pain; never push into a hard period.
4. What are you looking forward to, in the coming weeks or months?
   (next: And further ahead?)
5. If you compare a happy moment with a worth-living moment, is there a difference for you?

Done when: two or three moments with their feelings in the user's words, one look-forward, and (if safe) one hard-period source.

## Feelings vocabulary (offer only if the user struggles to name it)
calm, joy, pride, love, gratitude, hope, connection, absorption, aliveness, being needed, being yourself.

## Grounding to use here
- Before question 1: the source-and-feeling line.
- After question 3: the effort or hardship line.
- After question 4: the future-orientation line.

## Record in the profile
`ikigai_kan.moments` (what happened, feelings, body), `ikigai_kan.hard_period` (if shared), `ikigai_kan.looking_forward`.
