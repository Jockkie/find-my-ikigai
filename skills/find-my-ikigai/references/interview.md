# Interview

Each numbered item below is one message. Where an item lists several questions, ask the first and keep the rest for the next messages. Follow the energy: when an answer is alive, ask one more about it before moving on. Mirror back with their words.

## Part 1 · Where you stand

1. What brings you here now?
   (next: What happened that made you start asking this?)
2. Describe an ordinary week.
   (next: Where do you feel most like yourself?)
3. What gives you energy, even when it is hard work?
4. What drains you, even when it looks good from the outside?
5. When did you last forget the time?
6. What did you love doing as a child, before anyone told you what to become?
7. If nothing changed for five years, how would that feel?
8. Name the five things that matter most to you in life (values).
   (then, one value at a time: Whose voice first said this mattered?)

Done when: you know their current situation, what energises and drains them, the trigger moment, and their top values with the origin of each. Sources of meaning come in Part 3.

## Language to notice (never interpret without asking)

- "Should", "must", "have to", "is expected" often point to an external standard.
- "Want", "love", "can't stop", "curious" often point to an inner pull.
- Another person's name or voice attached to a wish.
- A sudden change in pace, breath or energy in how they write or speak about something.

Share what you notice only after asking: "May I tell you what I notice in how you describe these?"
